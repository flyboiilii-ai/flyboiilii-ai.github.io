Brand strategy · 45-page brand system · hospitality

Escape Away Hospitality: one promise, told to two audiences

A hospitality operator managing 400+ furnished units across Cairo, Sheikh Zayed, the North Coast, El Alamein and Gouna. The operation had outgrown the way it explained itself.

The real problem wasn't design

Escape Away sells to guests and to property owners at once. Guests want to feel something; owners want to feel safe. Before designing a page, I settled the strategy: one promise, two proofs.

Narrative, not page order

Four acts (About, Services, Portfolio, Process), each closing one objection in sequence, so a reader who stops halfway still leaves with the argument.

A sub-brand structure

City Escapes, Seaside Stays and Family & Group Stays for guests, plus Holiday Escapes and City Living, so one company speaks to a family in Gouna and a professional in New Cairo without blurring either message.

Designed proof

Operational data turned into pages that read in three seconds: the growth curve, the portfolio mix, and the eight-step journey from property intake to owner reporting.

“More Than a Stay. A Better Way to Host.”

The line the whole company now says the same way. The deck became the default answer to “what do you do?” in owner meetings, developer conversations and the first reply to an enquiry.

AI voice agent · lead qualification · real estate

Sandra: an AI agent that qualifies real-estate leads in Egyptian Arabic

Most real-estate companies don't lack leads; they lack a way to tell which ones are serious. I built the first-call layer: a voice agent that calls, asks the right questions, and hands sales a clean record.

What I designed

The persona and tone (Egyptian colloquial, consistent “حضرتك”), the opening and permission step, the qualification questions and their order, confirm-after-every-answer logic, the data fields, and the hand-off to sales.

Tested on a real call, weaknesses included

It handled an off-script question and returned to the flow, and turned “20% down payment” into “about a million” against the stated budget. It also talked over me twice, and the call ended mid-summary. Both are on the fix list.
