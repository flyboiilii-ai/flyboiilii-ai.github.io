El Hoshy Dental

A clinic site where each treatment explains itself in 3D.

People fear what they can't picture, so the site shows it. Four scroll-driven 3D sequences walk through a veneer, an implant, clear aligners and a full-mouth restoration, step by step, in English and in right-to-left Arabic. Every button opens a ready-written WhatsApp message to book.

  • Next.js
  • three.js
  • Blender + Python
  • GSAP
  • Arabic RTL

One fear per treatment

Each 3D sequence answers the question patients ask before a veneer, an implant or aligners. The five tooth and jaw models are scripted in Blender with Python, 43 to 525 KB each, so they can be rebuilt for any clinic.

Arabic that reads right to left, all of it

The Arabic site is a full mirror, not a translated layer: right-to-left layout, its own typeface, and a before and after slider that wipes from right to left.

Fast on a phone

three.js, GSAP and smooth scrolling load only after the first scroll, and older phones get still renders instead of WebGL. Google's PageSpeed test gave the mobile home page 96 on 18 October 2026.

JobPilot

Every point has a reason

Role 30, skills 30, experience 15, location 10, Web3 10 and AI 5, minus stated penalties. Missing requirements are quoted from the post. A local AI model can move a score by 10 points at most, and its reasons are labelled.

Claims it can't back up are blocked

Every CV, cover letter and form answer goes through a checker: figures, skills, @handles and names must trace to my profile or to the job post, or the text is rejected.

It knows where to stop

It never solves or bypasses a CAPTCHA, never automates LinkedIn, and never ticks a statement about me that my profile can't back up. When a site needs a person, it hands over the window.

Escape Away

One promise, told to two audiences.

An operator managing 400+ furnished units across Cairo, Sheikh Zayed, the North Coast, El Alamein and Gouna. The operation had outgrown the way it explained itself.

  • Positioning & voice
  • Narrative

The same system on Instagram · carousel series for El Gouna stays, October 2026

The real problem wasn't design

Escape Away sells to guests and to property owners at once. Guests want to feel something; owners want to feel safe. Before designing a page, I settled the strategy: one promise, two proofs.

Narrative, not page order

Four acts (About, Services, Portfolio, Process), each closing one objection in sequence, so a reader who stops halfway still leaves with the argument.

Designed proof

Operational data turned into pages that read in three seconds: the growth curve, the portfolio mix, and the eight-step journey from property intake to owner reporting.

The line the whole company now says the same way. The deck became the default answer to "what do you do?" in owner meetings, developer conversations and the first reply to an enquiry.
